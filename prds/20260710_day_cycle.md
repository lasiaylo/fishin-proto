# PRD: Day Cycle + Prestige
## Context

The game right now provides only one way to progress through shop upgrades. What would be more fun would be to have a meta-progression system that allows players another layer to strategize: prestiging.
We will have prestige contextualized through a day cycle. This will give players a better sense of rhythm and more decisions, namely:
- How do I get more out of one day? 
- What prestige upgrades can I look forward to?

## Requirements
- Day Timer:
  - Each day will last 15 minutes.
  - There is a progress bar in the InventoryView showing how much of the day has elapsed (it fills up over the day, rather than draining down).
  - The bar's fill color shifts from a dark, subtle tone to a brighter gray as the day nears completion, drawing more attention the closer the day is to ending.
  - Under this progress bar, have text showing the part of the day:
    - Night (0-5 min)
    - Dawn (5-12 min)
    - Sunrise (12-15 min)
  - The day cannot end while the player is in the middle of a fish fight — the transition is deferred until the fight resolves (win or loss), then happens immediately.
- Prestiging:
  - The player's total cumulative money earned (lifetime, not reset between days) contributes to earning a prestige point (we'll name them dream points for now).
  - Earning a prestige point will be similar to earning levels on a lure. There will be cumulative money earned requirements in order to earn dream points.
  - Dream points can be spent on dream upgrades, that are only available for purchase at the end of day.
  - The Dream Upgrade view will be seen on the same popup as the End of Day
  - The initial Dream Upgrade catalog is a single upgrade: **Improve Income** — each level increases the money earned per fish sold by 10%.
- End of Day Popup:
  - At the end of each day, show a popup with the following tabs:
    - Report
    - Dream Upgrades
  - The popup's backdrop is fully opaque black; the popup card itself fades in rather than appearing instantly.
  - End of Day Report:
    - At the end of each day, show a popup list summarizing the achievements for the day:
      - Fish caught
      - Amount of money earned
      - The player's dream-point level, labeled **LVL** — shown with a progress bar (and a "current / next" numeric readout) toward the next threshold, since it's the same lifetime, never-decreasing kind of level as a lure's.
    - All of the above count up from zero each time the popup opens, one after another (fish caught, then money earned, then LVL, then its progress bar) rather than all animating at once.
    - While on the Report tab, the popup's action button reads "dream" and switches to the Dream Upgrades tab instead of ending the day; once on that tab, the button reads "start next day".
  - Dream Upgrades:
    - Similar to ShopView, but instead shows purchases for dream upgrades.
    - We'll need another shopGameplay and shopDisplay csv, but for dream upgrades.
  - Starting the next day clears the event log and returns the player to the pond tab.
- Economy:
  - We'll need to make sure dream points and upgrades — including the Improve Income multiplier's effect on fish earnings — are logged properly in the game economy.
  - We may need to do a refactor of the Economy tab to reduce code re-use. There seems to be logic duplication for any type of Zustand store, such as the ShopStore
- Debug: Day cycle testing
  - Pressing ESC immediately ends the current day (regardless of how much time is left) and triggers the End of Day popup, unless a fish fight is in progress — in which case it takes effect the moment the fight ends.
  - Pressing Tab advances the day timer forward by 5 minutes, for quickly reaching a given day phase or the day's end without waiting.
  - These are debugging tools only, to let us reach the End of Day flow without waiting out the full 15 minutes.

## Solution

**Day timer**: `dayStore` tracks wall-clock day progress independently of the fishing loop — `dayStartTime`, `dayNumber`, and `isEndOfDay`. `DAY_DURATION_MS` (15 min) and the three phase boundaries (5 / 12 / 15 min) live in `constants.ts`. The `DayPhase` (`NIGHT` / `DAWN` / `SUNRISE`) is *not* stored — it's purely derived from elapsed time via an exported pure function, `phaseForElapsed(elapsed)`, since only `InventoryView` ever needs it and it's cheap to recompute on every render. `InventoryView` renders a `Progress` bar (same primitive as `StatBar`) plus the phase label underneath, driven by a local interval that recomputes elapsed time from `dayStartTime` and calls `dayStore.tickDay(now)` — the timer itself is derived, not stored as ticking state, so it survives re-renders and tab switches cleanly. The bar shows *elapsed* percentage (empty at day start, full at day's end) and its indicator color is overridden via a CSS `--accent-track` custom property set to `color-mix(in srgb, var(--gray-11) {elapsedPct}%, var(--gray-6))` — a continuous blend from a dark, subtle tone to a bright, prominent gray as the day nears completion.

**Dream points as a threshold system**: dream points are earned from the player's **lifetime cumulative money earned** crossing a series of thresholds — not the money earned in the current day. This makes dream points structurally identical (not just similar) to how lure levels are earned from cumulative XP (`LURE_LEVEL_XP` / `computeLureLevel` / `applyLureXp` in `constants.ts`): both are monotonically-increasing lifetime counters that never reset. Rather than duplicate that logic, `computeLureLevel` and `applyLureXp` are generalized into threshold-agnostic `computeLevelFromThresholds(thresholds, value)` / `applyThresholdGain(thresholds, current, gain)`, and both lure XP and dream points become thin callers of the shared function with their own threshold arrays (`LURE_LEVEL_XP` vs. a new `DREAM_POINT_MONEY_THRESHOLDS`). `dayStore` subscribes to `usePlayer`'s `wallet` field directly (the same "did the wallet just go up" diff `InventoryView` already does for its `+amount` popup) so no call sites in `PondView` need to know about dream points — any wallet increase, from any source, feeds a lifetime `cumulativeMoneyEarned` counter and is run through the threshold function to award dream points. The day boundary is irrelevant to *earning* dream points: they accrue continuously as money is earned, day or no day. Only *spending* them is gated to the End of Day popup. `moneyEarnedToday` is tracked separately, purely for the Report tab, and resets every day; it is not an input to the dream point calculation. In the Report tab this level is relabeled **LVL** (to read as a permanent milestone rather than a spendable count) and paired with `computeDreamPointsProgress(cumulativeMoneyEarned)` — returning `{ current, next, fraction }` — to drive a progress bar and a "`{current} / {next}`" readout, the same level-plus-progress-bar presentation already used for lure leveling in `PondView`. This is deliberately independent of the *spendable* `dreamPoints` balance (which the Dream Upgrades tab still prices against) — LVL reflects lifetime earnings and never drops when points are spent.

**Reducing Zustand store duplication**: `shopStore` and the new dream-upgrade store need almost identical logic (load CSV catalog → track levels → price lookup → afford check → buy → debug level overrides → persist to `localStorage`), differing only in *which currency pays for it* and *which CSV catalog backs it*. Rather than copy-pasting `shopStore.ts`, that logic is extracted into a `createUpgradeStore(options)` factory — parameterized by a currency accessor/deductor pair and a `localStorage` key — that both `shopStore` (paying with `wallet`) and the new `dreamShopStore` (paying with `dreamPoints`) call to produce their store + actions. The stat-application switch (`LURE`, `HP`, `INVENTORY`, `INCOME`, `ROD`, `ROD_SLOT`, `ROD_ATTACK`, `ROD_DEFENSE`, `BAIT`) is shared verbatim, since dream upgrades apply to the same underlying player stats as regular shop upgrades — they're just gated behind a different currency and only purchasable from the End-of-Day popup. The grouping/pips presentation itself is also shared: `UpgradeCatalogGrid` is a standalone component (taking a catalog, currency, and price/afford/buy callbacks as props) used by both `ShopView` and the End of Day popup's Dream Upgrades tab, rather than being duplicated between them.

**Dream Upgrades catalog — Improve Income**: the initial catalog is a single upgrade, `DREAM_INCOME` (`DreamShopGameplay.csv` / `DreamShopDisplay.csv`, same column shape as the regular shop's CSVs, loaded through the same `loadShopData`/`parseShopGameplayRows` code path in `csvLoader.ts` generalized to take a `folder` + `displayFile` override instead of hardcoding `Shop`/`ShopDisplay.csv`). It uses a new `INCOME` stat name and a new `incomeBoostPercent` field on `PlayerStats`, incremented by the upgrade's `valuePerLevel` (10, i.e. +10%) per level — the same generic `delta * valuePerLevel` pattern already used for `HP`/`INVENTORY`. `PondView.finishFight` applies the resulting multiplier at the moment of sale: `effectivePrice = fish.basePrice * (1 + incomeBoostPercent / 100)`. `EconomyModel.ts` mirrors this in every fish-earnings computation (`evalLure`, `computeLureStats`, and the bait-rod income block in `simulateEconomy`) via an `incomeMultiplier` derived the same way, per the sync rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

**End of Day popup**: when `dayStore.isEndOfDay` flips true, all active rod loops in `PondView` pause (no new casts/bites/timeouts tick) until the player dismisses the popup — mirroring how a rod slot already goes idle when its assignment is "None". A new `EndOfDayPopup` component renders as a fixed, fully-opaque-black overlay; the popup card itself uses the app's existing `.fade-in` animation (the same one `PondView`/`ActionsSection` already use for tab transitions) instead of appearing instantly. It contains a *controlled* Radix `Tabs.Root`:
- **Report**: fish caught, money earned, and LVL (with its progress bar), all read from `dayStore`. Each of these uses a shared `AnimatedNumber` component (backed by a `useAnimatedNumber` hook) that counts up from an explicit `initial={0}` rather than showing the final value immediately — the same rAF-counting approach `InventoryView` already used for its wallet display, now extracted so both places share it. The hook also accepts a `delayMs`, and each stat's expected animation length (via an exported `animationDuration(delta)` helper) is chained into the next stat's delay, so fish caught animates, then money earned, then LVL, then its progress bar — one after another instead of all four running in parallel.
- **Dream Upgrades**: visually identical to `ShopView` (both render `UpgradeCatalogGrid`) but backed by `dreamShopStore` and priced in dream points instead of `CURRENCY_SYMBOL`.

Because the tab state is controlled, the popup's footer button can react to which tab is active: on Report it reads "dream" and clicking it switches to the Dream Upgrades tab; once there, it reads "start next day" and calls `startNewDay()`.

`startNewDay()` resets `dayStartTime`, `moneyEarnedToday`, `fishCaughtToday`, and `isEndOfDay`, increments `dayNumber`, un-pauses rods, clears the event log (`eventLogStore.clearEvents()`), and — via `ActionsSection` watching `dayNumber` and resetting its own local tab state — returns the player to the pond tab. `cumulativeMoneyEarned`, dream points, and any dream upgrades already bought persist across the transition — nothing about the wallet, inventory, or regular shop progress resets at a day boundary; only the day timer, the day's own earnings/catch counters, the event log, and the active UI tab do.

**Debug: force-ending the day, advancing time, and never interrupting a fight**: an ESC keydown listener calls `forceEndDay()`, and a Tab keydown listener calls `advanceTime(5 * 60 * 1000)` — both live alongside the existing backtick-toggles-the-debug-panel listener in `App.jsx`'s single keydown handler rather than introducing new listener patterns (Tab's default focus-shifting behavior is suppressed with `preventDefault()`). Neither takes effect immediately if a fish fight is in progress: `dayStore` tracks a `fightingSlots` set of rod slot indices currently mid-fight (set via `setFighting(slotIndex, fighting)`, called by `PondView` when a fight starts and again once it resolves — win, loss, or component unmount as a safety net) plus a sticky `endOfDayRequested` flag. `tickDay` (the natural timer), `forceEndDay`, and `advanceTime` all set `endOfDayRequested = true` and then call a shared `applyEndOfDayIfReady()`, which only flips `isEndOfDay` once requested *and* `fightingSlots` is empty — so the request is remembered and applied the instant the last active fight concludes, rather than being silently dropped or interrupting the fight. `advanceTime` additionally pulls `dayStartTime` backward by the given amount and re-runs `tickDay` immediately so a debug jump across a day boundary (or across a phase boundary) takes effect without waiting for the next 250ms poll.

```
PondView (wallet +$) ──► usePlayer wallet diff ──► dayStore.recordEarnings()
                                                         │
                                              moneyEarnedToday += diff        (Report-only, resets daily)
                                              cumulativeMoneyEarned += diff   (lifetime, never resets)
                                              dreamPoints += thresholdsCrossed(cumulativeMoneyEarned)
                                                         │
                        elapsed >= DAY_DURATION_MS  ──┐  │  ┌── forceEndDay() / advanceTime()  (ESC / Tab)
                                                       ▼  ▼  ▼
                                          endOfDayRequested = true
                                                         │
                                     fightingSlots.size === 0? ──── no ──► deferred until setFighting(_, false)
                                                         │ yes
                                                         ▼
                                              dayStore.isEndOfDay = true
                                                         │
                                        PondView rods pause · EndOfDayPopup fades in (black backdrop)
                                                 ┌───────┴────────┐
                                            Report tab      Dream Upgrades tab
                                    (fish/$/LVL, counting   (dreamShopStore, Improve
                                     up in sequence)         Income, paid in dream points)
                                                         │
                                    "dream" (Report) ──► switches tab
                                    "start next day" (Dream Upgrades) ──► startNewDay()
                                                         │
                                    clears event log · resets ActionsSection to pond tab
```

## Implementation Plan

### Phase 1 — Threshold generalization + constants
**`src/util/constants.ts`**:
- Extract `computeLevelFromThresholds(thresholds, value)` and `applyThresholdGain(thresholds, current, gain)` from the existing `computeLureLevel` / `applyLureXp` bodies. Reimplement `computeLureLevel` / `applyLureXp` as thin wrappers around these so lure-leveling behavior is unchanged.
- Add `DAY_DURATION_MS = 15 * 60 * 1000`.
- Add `DayPhase` enum (`NIGHT`, `DAWN`, `SUNRISE`) and `DAY_PHASE_BOUNDARIES_MS = [5, 12, 15].map(m => m * 60 * 1000)`.
- Add `DREAM_POINT_MONEY_THRESHOLDS` (array of cumulative-money requirements, same shape as `LURE_LEVEL_XP`) and a `computeDreamPoints(cumulativeMoneyEarned)` helper built on `computeLevelFromThresholds`, evaluated against lifetime earnings, not a per-day counter. Later joined by `computeDreamPointsProgress(cumulativeMoneyEarned)`, returning `{ current, next, fraction }` for the Report tab's LVL progress bar.

### Phase 2 — `dayStore`
**`src/stores/dayStore.ts`** (new): state — `dayNumber`, `dayStartTime`, `moneyEarnedToday`, `cumulativeMoneyEarned`, `fishCaughtToday`, `dreamPoints`, `isEndOfDay`.
- `tickDay(now)` — checks `now - dayStartTime` against `DAY_DURATION_MS` and requests an end-of-day if elapsed (see the fight-gating design below); does not track `DayPhase` itself, since that's a pure function of elapsed time (`phaseForElapsed`, exported alongside) needed only by `InventoryView`.
- `recordEarnings(amount)` — bumps both `moneyEarnedToday` (Report-tab display only) and `cumulativeMoneyEarned` (lifetime). Recomputes dream points by diffing `computeDreamPoints(cumulativeMoneyEarned)` before/after the bump (same before/after-level-diff shape as `applyLureXp`) and adds the delta to the spendable `dreamPoints` balance.
- `recordCatch()` — increments `fishCaughtToday`.
- `deductDreamPoints(amount)` — decrements the spendable `dreamPoints` balance only; does not touch `cumulativeMoneyEarned`, so spending never claws back already-earned lifetime progress.
- `forceEndDay()` / `advanceTime(ms)` — debug-only entry points (ESC / Tab); see Phase 10 for how these and the natural timer defer to an in-progress fight instead of ending the day directly.
- `startNewDay()` — resets `dayStartTime`, `moneyEarnedToday`, `fishCaughtToday`, `isEndOfDay`; increments `dayNumber`. Leaves `cumulativeMoneyEarned` and `dreamPoints` untouched — dream point progress is lifetime and crosses day boundaries freely. Later joined by clearing the event log (Phase 12).
- On module init, `usePlayer.subscribe((s) => s.wallet, (wallet, prevWallet) => { if (wallet > prevWallet) recordEarnings(wallet - prevWallet); })` — reuses the increase-only diff pattern already in `InventoryView`.

### Phase 3 — Generic upgrade store factory
**`src/stores/upgradeStoreFactory.ts`** (new): pull the body of `shopStore.ts` (`initShopFromData`, `getUpgradePrice`, `canAffordUpgrade`, `isMaxed`, `buyUpgrade`, `setUpgradeLevelDebug`, `resetAllUpgradesDebug`, `localStorage` persistence) into `createUpgradeStore({ storageKey, getCurrency, deductCurrency })`, returning the same set of functions bound to that currency. The stat-application switch (`LURE`/`HP`/`INVENTORY`/`ROD`/`ROD_SLOT`/`ROD_ATTACK`/`ROD_DEFENSE`/`BAIT`, later joined by `INCOME` in Phase 11) moves into a shared `applyStatEffect` used by both call sites.
- **`src/stores/shopStore.ts`**: becomes a thin instantiation — `createUpgradeStore({ storageKey: "debug_upgrade_levels", getCurrency: getWallet, deductCurrency: deductMoney })` — re-exporting the returned functions under their existing names so no import sites change.

### Phase 4 — Dream shop data + store
**`public/data/Dream/DreamShopGameplay.csv`** (new) and **`public/data/Dream/DreamShopDisplay.csv`** (new): same column shape as `ShopGameplay.csv` / `ShopDisplay.csv`. (Initially seeded with a placeholder multi-upgrade catalog; replaced in Phase 11 with the single `DREAM_INCOME` upgrade.)
**`src/util/csvLoader.ts`**: generalize `loadShopData` / `loadShopGameplayData` to accept a `{ folder, displayFile }` override (defaulting to today's `Shop` / `ShopDisplay.csv`) instead of hardcoding the path, so the same functions load the Dream catalog.
**`src/stores/dreamShopStore.ts`** (new): `createUpgradeStore({ storageKey: "debug_dream_upgrade_levels", getCurrency: () => useDayStore.getState().dreamPoints, deductCurrency: deductDreamPoints })`, loaded from the Dream CSV pair via the generalized loader, using the `deductDreamPoints` action added to `dayStore` in Phase 2.

### Phase 5 — Day timer UI
**`src/components/InventoryView.tsx`**: add a day-timer block above the wallet display — a `Progress` bar recomputed from `dayStartTime` on a local interval (same interval driving `dayStore.tickDay`), with the current `DayPhase` label beneath it. (Later changed in Phase 14 to show elapsed rather than remaining time, and to shift color from dark to gray as the day nears its end.)

### Phase 6 — Catch/earnings hooks
**`src/components/PondView.tsx`**: in `finishFight`, on a win, call `dayStore.recordCatch()` alongside the existing `addLureXp` / `consumeBait` side effects.
- Gate each rod row's cast/bite/reel ticking on `!useDayStore((s) => s.isEndOfDay)` — same shape as the existing "rod slot assigned to None" pause check — so fishing freezes the instant the day ends and resumes after `startNewDay()`.

### Phase 7 — End of Day popup
**`src/components/EndOfDayPopup.tsx`** (new): fixed-position overlay, rendered from `App.jsx` when `useDayStore((s) => s.isEndOfDay)` is true (same overlay pattern as the existing debug panel). Radix `Tabs.Root` with:
- **Report tab**: reads `moneyEarnedToday` / `fishCaughtToday` from `dayStore`, rendered as a simple summary list.
- **Dream Upgrades tab**: same grouping/`LevelPips` presentation as `ShopView`, sourced from `dreamShopStore`'s upgrades and `dreamPoints` balance instead of `wallet`.
- "Start Next Day" button calling `startNewDay()`.

**`src/App.jsx`**: extend the existing `handleKey` keydown listener (currently only handling `` ` `` for the debug panel) with an `"Escape"` case that calls `dayStore.forceEndDay()`.

(Phases 12/13/16 below substantially extend this component: a controlled-tab dream/start-next-day button swap, animated/sequenced Report stats and LVL progress bar, and a black backdrop with a fade-in card.)

### Phase 8 — Economy Model
**`src/game/EconomyModel.ts`** (per the sync rule in `CLAUDE.md` — this mechanic changes both timing structure and money flow, so the model must track it):
- `simulateEconomy` gains a day-boundary check: track `cumulativeTime % DAY_DURATION_MS` (or an explicit `dayElapsed` accumulator alongside the existing `cumulativeTime`) purely to know when to open the simulated End of Day popup and let dream upgrades be "purchased." A separate `cumulativeMoneyEarned` running total — the sum of `income` across every round so far, never reset — is what dream points are computed from via `computeDreamPoints`; a per-day `moneyEarnedToday` total is kept only for the `EconomyRound` snapshot/report parity, mirroring `dayStore`.
- Add `dreamShopData: ShopUpgradeData[]` and `dreamPoints` tracking, mirroring the existing `levels` / `wallet` tracking for the regular shop.
- Generalize `cheapestUpgrade` / `pickUpgrade` / `applyUpgrade` to take a `(catalog, levels, currency)` tuple rather than being hardcoded to the wallet shop, so the same functions drive dream-upgrade purchasing at each simulated day boundary (always buy the cheapest affordable dream upgrade, same heuristic as the existing wallet strategy).
- **`EconomyRound`**: add `dayNumber: number`, `cumulativeMoneyEarned: number`, `dreamPoints: number`, `moneyEarnedToday: number`, `dreamUpgradesBought: string[]`.
- Extended in Phase 11 to apply the `INCOME` stat's multiplier (`incomeBoostPercent`) to every fish-earnings computation (`evalLure`, `computeLureStats`, the bait-rod income block), mirroring `PondView`'s sale-time multiplier.

### Phase 9 — CSV Generator
**`src/components/model/CsvGenerator.tsx`**: add a `DreamShop` generation section mirroring the existing `generateShopRows`/`ShopGenerator` UI (its own `FunctionConfig` price curve, tier count, and "Download DreamShopGameplay.csv" button), so dream-upgrade pricing can be balanced the same way regular shop pricing is today. (Simplified in Phase 11 to a single price-curve-plus-tier-count control for `DREAM_INCOME`, matching the reduced catalog.)

### Phase 10 — Trim `dayStore`
`phase` is removed from `DayState` entirely — it was purely derived from `dayStartTime`, had no internal consumer, and was read by exactly one component (`InventoryView`). `phaseForElapsed(elapsed)` becomes an exported pure function computed inline wherever needed; `tickPhase` is renamed `tickDay` since it no longer touches phase, only the elapsed-vs-`DAY_DURATION_MS` check. The remaining fields (`dayNumber`, `dayStartTime`, `moneyEarnedToday`, `cumulativeMoneyEarned`, `fishCaughtToday`) stay in the store even though most are also read externally in only one place, since — unlike `phase` — they're accumulators mutated by store actions with no other value to derive them from.

### Phase 11 — Replace the dream catalog with a single Improve Income upgrade
Removes the placeholder 5-entry dream catalog (HP/inventory/rod stats/rod slot) in favor of one upgrade. Adds `StatName.INCOME` and `PlayerStats.incomeBoostPercent`, wires `INCOME` through `applyStatEffect` (`upgradeStoreFactory.ts`) the same way `HP`/`INVENTORY` work, applies the resulting multiplier at sale time in `PondView.finishFight`, and mirrors it in `EconomyModel`'s `cheapestUpgrade`/`applyUpgrade` plus all three fish-earnings computations. `DreamShopGameplay.csv`/`DreamShopDisplay.csv` and the `CsvGenerator`'s Dream Shop section are both reduced to the single `DREAM_INCOME` entry.

### Phase 12 — End of Day popup: tab-switching button, LVL + progress bar, sequenced animations, styling
- `EndOfDayPopup`'s `Tabs.Root` becomes controlled (`useState` + `onValueChange`) so the footer button can read the active tab: "dream" on Report (switches to Dream Upgrades), "start next day" on Dream Upgrades (calls `startNewDay()`).
- The "dream points" row is relabeled **LVL**, showing `computeDreamPoints(cumulativeMoneyEarned)` with a `Progress` bar and a "`{current} / {next}`" label from `computeDreamPointsProgress`.
- `AnimatedNumber`/`useAnimatedNumber` (`src/components/AnimatedNumber.tsx`, new) extract the rAF count-up logic previously inline in `InventoryView`'s wallet display, adding an `initial` starting value and a `delayMs` plus an exported `animationDuration(delta)` helper. `InventoryView`'s wallet display is refactored to use it. In the Report tab, fish caught, money earned, LVL, and the LVL progress bar's fill percentage all animate via this hook, each one's delay computed from the predicted duration of the ones before it, so they run in sequence rather than in parallel.
- The overlay backdrop becomes fully opaque black (`#000`, was a translucent `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`); the popup card gets the app's existing `.fade-in` class instead of appearing instantly.

### Phase 13 — Day progress bar: count up and shift color
`InventoryView`'s day-timer bar switches from showing time *remaining* (full at day start, draining down) to time *elapsed* (empty at day start, filling up). Its indicator color is overridden via an inline `--accent-track: color-mix(in srgb, var(--gray-11) {elapsedPct}%, var(--gray-6))`, continuously blending from a dark, subtle tone to a bright, prominent gray as the day nears completion.

### Phase 14 — Debug: advance time
`dayStore.advanceTime(ms)` pulls `dayStartTime` backward by `ms` and re-runs `tickDay` immediately (rather than waiting for the next poll). Wired to a `Tab` keydown in `App.jsx`'s existing debug handler (`e.preventDefault()` to stop the browser's default focus-shifting behavior), advancing the day by 5 minutes per press — useful for quickly reaching a given phase or the day's end without waiting out the timer.

### Phase 15 — Start next day: clear event log, reset to pond tab
`eventLogStore` gains `clearEvents()`, called from `dayStore.startNewDay()` so the day-transition action is the single place resetting day-scoped state. `ActionsSection` watches `dayStore`'s `dayNumber` and resets its local tab state to `"pond"` whenever it changes, since the active tab is component-local UI state that `startNewDay()` can't reach directly.

### Phase 16 — Don't end the day mid-fight
`dayStore` tracks a `fightingSlots` set of rod slot indices currently mid-fight (via a new `setFighting(slotIndex, fighting)` action) and a sticky `endOfDayRequested` flag. `tickDay`, `forceEndDay`, and `advanceTime` all set `endOfDayRequested = true` and call a shared `applyEndOfDayIfReady()`, which only flips `isEndOfDay` once requested *and* `fightingSlots` is empty. `PondView` calls `setFighting(slotIndex, true)` when a fight starts and `setFighting(slotIndex, false)` once it resolves (win or loss) or on unmount (safety net), so a pending end-of-day request — from the natural timer or either debug hotkey — takes effect the instant the last active fight concludes instead of interrupting it.
